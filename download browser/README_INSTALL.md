## 📱 Android Installation Guide (Realme Note 60, Redmi 5, etc.)

1. **Transfer or Download:**
   - Copy `GB-Browser.apk` to your phone via USB cable, Google Drive, or direct download.
2. **Enable Unknown Sources / Install Unknown Apps:**
   - On **Realme Note 60 / Realme UI**: Go to *Settings → Security → Unknown Source Installation → Allow*.
   - On **Redmi 5 / MIUI**: Go to *Settings → Additional settings → Privacy → Special app access → Install unknown apps → Enable*.
3. **Install & Launch:**
   - Open your file manager, tap `GB-Browser.apk`, and tap **Install**.
   - Tap **Open** to enjoy ultra-fast browsing!

## 🍏 iPhone / iOS Installation Guide (iOS 14 to iOS 26+)

### Method 1: Sideloadly (Recommended for PC/Mac)
1. Download **Sideloadly** on your Mac or PC from [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io).
2. Connect your iPhone via USB.
3. Drag `GB-Browser.ipa` into Sideloadly.
4. Enter your Apple ID and click **Start**.
5. On your iPhone: Go to *Settings → General → VPN & Device Management*, tap your Apple ID, and tap **Trust**.

### Method 2: AltStore
1. Install **AltServer** on your computer from [altstore.io](https://altstore.io).
2. Install AltStore on your iOS device.
3. AirDrop or copy `GB-Browser.ipa` to your iPhone Files app.
4. Open AltStore → My Apps → `+` icon → Select `GB-Browser.ipa` to install.

### Method 3: TrollStore (Jailbreak / CoreTrust bypass devices)
- Open `GB-Browser.ipa` directly in TrollStore for permanent signing without 7-day expiration.
